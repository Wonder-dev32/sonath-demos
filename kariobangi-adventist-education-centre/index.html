About us

Welcome to Kariobangi Adventist Education Centre

At Kariobangi Adventist Education Centre, we believe that every child is unique and deserving of a high-quality education. Our experienced teachers and staff work together to create a personalized learning experience that meets the needs of each student. We strive to inspire a love of learning, promote social responsibility, and develop the skills and confidence needed to succeed in life.
