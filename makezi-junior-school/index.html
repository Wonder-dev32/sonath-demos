About us

Welcome to Makezi Junior School

We are a vibrant community of learners, committed to providing a well-rounded education that prepares our students for success in all areas of life. Our curriculum is designed to meet the needs of each child, and our small class sizes ensure that every student receives the attention they deserve. We believe that every child has the potential to excel, and we are dedicated to helping them achieve their goals.
