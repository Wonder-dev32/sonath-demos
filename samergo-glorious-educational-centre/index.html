About us

Welcome to Samergo Glorious Educational Centre

Our school is built on the principles of academic excellence, character development, and community engagement. We strive to create a warm and inclusive atmosphere where students feel valued and supported. Our experienced staff are dedicated to helping each child achieve their unique goals and aspirations.

Academic Excellence

Our rigorous curriculum and experienced teachers ensure students achieve their full potential.

Personal Growth

We foster a supportive environment that encourages students to develop their unique talents and character.

Community Engagement

Our school is deeply connected to the local community, promoting social responsibility and civic engagement.

Our programmes

Pre-School

A nurturing environment for children aged 3-5, fostering social, emotional, and cognitive development.

Primary

A well-rounded education for children aged 6-11, emphasizing academic rigor, creativity, and critical thinking.

Secondary

A challenging and supportive program for students aged 12-18, preparing them for success in higher education and beyond.

Pre-Vocational

A practical and skills-based program for students aged 15-18, equipping them with the knowledge and skills for the modern workforce.
