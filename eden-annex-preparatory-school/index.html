About us

Welcome to Eden Annex Preparatory School

At Eden Annex, we believe in the unique potential of every child. Our Kindergarten and Pre-Primary programs are designed to inspire creativity, encourage exploration, and develop essential skills for a lifelong love of learning. We strive to create a warm and welcoming community where children feel safe, valued, and supported as they grow and thrive.

Nurturing Excellence

Our experienced teachers provide individualized attention and support to help each child reach their full potential.

Inclusive Community

We welcome children from diverse backgrounds and abilities, creating a rich and inclusive learning environment.

Personalized Learning

Our programs are tailored to meet the unique needs and interests of each child, promoting a lifelong love of learning.
