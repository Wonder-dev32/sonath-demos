About us

Welcome to Ngota's Upendo Nursery School and Youth Center

At Ngota's Upendo, we believe that every child is unique and deserves a high-quality education that meets their individual needs. Our curriculum is designed to foster creativity, critical thinking, and a love of learning that will last a lifetime. We strive to create a warm and inclusive community that feels like a second family to our students and their families.
